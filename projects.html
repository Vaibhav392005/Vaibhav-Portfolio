Optimization

Brake Pedal Optimization

Designed and optimized a brake pedal system using CAD and simulation tools to improve structural performance.

Problem Statement

Traditional designs have excess material and uneven stress distribution.

Design Approach

  • 3D modeling in SolidWorks
  • Focused on load-bearing regions
  • Optimized geometry

Simulation

  • Performed ANSYS analysis
  • Checked deformation and stress

Outcome

Improved efficiency and optimized structural design.
